# Software Delivery Performance Learning Hour

A Samman Learning Hour that introduces DORA's software delivery performance metrics to a cross-team community of practice.

## Session

**Learning Hour**:
A roughly one-hour remote training session led by a facilitator, structured in four phases: Connect, Concept, Concrete Practice, Conclusions.
_Avoid_: Workshop, training, kata session

**Participant**:
A member of the community of practice attending the Learning Hour, assumed to have little prior knowledge of DORA metrics.
_Avoid_: Attendee, student, learner

**Facilitator**:
The person who runs the Learning Hour and follows the facilitation guide.
_Avoid_: Coach, presenter, trainer

**Facilitation Guide**:
The step-by-step how-to the Facilitator follows to prepare and run the Learning Hour.
_Avoid_: Runbook, script, facilitator notes

**Breakout Room**:
One of five remote sub-groups in Concrete Practice, each assigned exactly one DORA metric.
_Avoid_: Group, team, table

**Metric Card**:
The artifact a Breakout Room produces during Concrete Practice for one metric: definition in own words, how to measure it, what makes it go bad, and one practice that improves it.
_Avoid_: Sticky, template, worksheet

**Readout**:
A Breakout Room's roughly 3-minute presentation of its Metric Card back in the main room.
_Avoid_: Showcase, demo, report-back

## Software delivery performance

**Software Delivery Performance**:
How well a team delivers changes to an application or service, as measured by DORA's five metrics taken together.
_Avoid_: DevOps performance, velocity, productivity

**Throughput**:
A measure of how many changes can move through the system over a period of time: Change Lead Time, Deployment Frequency, Failed Deployment Recovery Time.
_Avoid_: Speed, velocity

**Instability**:
A measure of how well the software deployments go: Change Fail Rate, Deployment Rework Rate.
_Avoid_: Stability, quality

**Change Lead Time**:
The amount of time it takes for a change to go from committed to version control to deployed in production.
_Avoid_: Lead time for changes, cycle time

**Deployment Frequency**:
The number of deployments over a given period or the time between deployments.
_Avoid_: Release frequency, release cadence

**Failed Deployment Recovery Time**:
The time it takes to recover from a deployment that fails and requires immediate intervention.
_Avoid_: MTTR, mean time to restore, time to restore service

**Change Fail Rate**:
The ratio of deployments that require immediate intervention following a deployment.
_Avoid_: Change failure rate, failure rate

**Deployment Rework Rate**:
The ratio of deployments that are unplanned but happen as a result of an incident in production.
_Avoid_: Rework rate, hotfix rate
